1 · Scanning (daily, 15 minutes)

  • Liquidity gate: 30-day average traded value ≥ ₹3 cr (raise to ₹10-50 cr for size).
  • Trend template (Minervini Stage 2): price > 50 EMA > 200 EMA, 200 EMA rising, within 25% of the 52-week high — apply it at the breakout, not the launch low: the study shows most NSE winners begin below their 50 EMA inside a base, then clear the template as they launch.
  • Relative strength: 3-month return vs Nifty ≥ +15pp for the watchlist. Note the study's nuance: only ~47% of winners outran Nifty before launch — leadership often develops during the move — so treat RS as a quality filter, not a gate, and re-rank it weekly as moves develop.
  • Market gate: check the regime banner first. When Nifty is below its 200 EMA and breadth is thin, cut size or stand aside — most big winners launch in risk-on tapes.
  • Tag scan: run the setup tags below; anything with 2+ tags goes on the watchlist.

2 · Watchlist (living document)

  • Keep 15-25 names max: Stage-2 + RS-leader + an active setup tag.
  • Each entry needs a pivot price (the level that triggers the trade) and a stop level written down before the session.
  • Review nightly: remove names that break down or go stale (no progress in 2-3 weeks), add fresh breakouts.
  • Rank by setup quality: VCP coil > base breakout > handle/pullback > extended flags.
  • Watch sector clusters — 3+ winners from the same sector is a rotation signal.

3 · Setup catalog (the famous patterns, adapted for NSE)

  • VCP coil (Minervini): 2-4 progressively tighter pullbacks (e.g. −20%, −12%, −6%) on drying volume inside a Stage-2 uptrend. Entry: close above the pivot on ≥1.5-2× volume. Stop: below the final contraction low, 3-7% risk.
  • Cup & handle (O'Neil): rounded 12-30% deep base over 7-26 weeks, quiet handle in the upper third. Entry above handle high on a volume surge.
  • High tight flag (O'Neil/Minervini "Power Play"): +60-100% in 4-8 weeks, then a 3-5 week flag correcting <20% on quiet volume. Rare and explosive.
  • Qullamaggie continuation: +30-100% in 1-3 months, then an orderly 2-8 week consolidation with higher lows and tight range. Entry on range expansion / opening-range high.
  • EP / first pullback (Qullamaggie): after a fresh breakout, the first controlled pullback to the 10/21 EMA on below-average volume — the highest win-rate add.
  • Breakout & retest: breakout above a multi-week base, pullback to the breakout level on light volume, resume — safest entry for wider stops.

4 · Trading style for India

  • Primary: swing trading, 3-15 sessions. Indian large/mid-caps respect levels, delivery-based swing avoids F&O complexity and SEBI intraday margin rules, and STCG is a flat 20% — the most accessible active style for NSE retail.
  • Overlay: positional, 2-6 months. The study's continuation stats (see above) show +25% winners keep running — the biggest ones deserve a core position held with a 50-day trailing stop, sized so you can sit through 10-15% drawdowns.
  • Avoid as the main style: intraday (noise, margins, circuits) and long-term investing without the trend template (dead money in Stage 1/3 names).
  • India caveats: daily circuits (5/10/20%) can gap a stop through — keep position size such that a locked-limit day costs <1% of capital; prefer cash delivery for swing legs.

5 · Timeframes & risk

  • Weekly chart: regime and stage — is there a base, is the 200 EMA rising?
  • Daily chart: everything actionable — pivots, stops, volume confirmation. One daily close above/below a level carries more information than any intraday print.
  • Entry trigger (optional): the opening range or first 60-min candle of the breakout day — for timing only, never for the thesis.
  • Risk: 0.5-1% of capital per trade; stops 3-8% from entry; add only when a trade works (pyramid into strength); cut failed breakouts within 2-3 sessions — a breakout that round-trips is a signal, not a wrinkle.
  • Exits: swing leg into the first climactic range expansion or 10-21 EMA loss; positional core on a 50 EMA close.
